# Code of Conduct

## Our Pledge
We pledge to make participation in this community a welcoming, inclusive, and harassment-free experience for everyone, regardless of age, disability, ethnicity, gender identity and expression, experience, education, nationality, appearance, race, religion, or sexual identity and orientation.

## Our Standards
Positive behavior includes empathy, respect for differing viewpoints, constructive feedback, accepting responsibility, and focusing on what is best for the community. Unacceptable behavior includes harassment, insults, personal attacks, unwanted sexual attention, publishing private information without permission, and conduct inappropriate in a professional setting.

## Enforcement Responsibilities
Community leaders are responsible for clarifying and enforcing these standards and taking fair corrective action in response to inappropriate behavior.

## Scope
This Code applies in community spaces and when an individual officially represents the community in public spaces.

## Enforcement
Report abusive or unacceptable behavior to the community leaders responsible for enforcement. Complaints will be reviewed promptly and fairly, while respecting reporter privacy.

## Enforcement Guidelines
1. Correction: a private written warning.
2. Warning: a formal warning with consequences for continued behavior.
3. Temporary Ban: temporary restriction from community interaction.
4. Permanent Ban: permanent removal from the community.
